FrequencyWords 2018 lists, Hermit Dave, derived from OpenSubtitles2018.
Original dataset: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/tree/525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6/content/2018
License for content: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Ajadi Keys extracted up to 25000 high-frequency Russian tokens and up to 12000 English/Hebrew tokens, with language-script and token-format validation, NFC/lowercasing and tab-separated frequency counts. The adapted .tsv files remain CC BY-SA 4.0. No content censorship and no code from the upstream project.


Ajadi Keys context tables (*.akn), 0.9.0:
Derived aggregate word and adjacent word-pair/triple counts from Tatoeba contributors,
https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads and https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/
snapshot 2026-11-21, CC BY 2.0 FR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).
Vocabulary includes the existing FrequencyWords data above; the derived context
tables are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. No original sentences are shipped.
The tables were pruned, encoded as compact integer counts and indexed; see
qa/prediction/sources.json for source URLs and checksums and scripts/build_prediction_data.py.

Russian morphological tags and protection filter (ru.morph.tsv, ru.akp):
Derived from the OpenCorpora morphological dictionary (https://opencorpora.org/,
CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), as packaged in
pymorphy3-dicts-ru 2.4.417150.4580142. The build-time analyzer pymorphy3 2.0.6
is MIT; it does not ship in the Android APK. The Russian tag/protection data
are distributed CC BY-SA 3.0. The filter includes known OpenCorpora forms of
common lemmas plus validated forms observed in the training partition.
